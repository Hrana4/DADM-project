# DADM-project
Energy -Equity Nexus or correlation
Research Context
This dashboard was built as part of a thesis investigating the "Energy-Equity Nexus" — the strategic impact of renewable energy volatility on German industrial valuation. The Energiewende transition creates hourly fluctuations in Germany's renewable generation mix that may transmit into equity market uncertainty for energy-intensive firms through the electricity price channel.
Key mechanism: Low renewable feed-in → higher spot electricity prices (EPEX Spot) → compressed margins for energy-intensive firms → investor repricing → elevated intraday volatility.
